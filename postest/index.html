Postest

Postest is the in-house publishing tool of Indaga Studios, a small independent comics and animation studio. We use it to plan, review and publish our own content (comics, process videos, short animations) to the studio's own creator accounts on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

What it does

  • Plan posts on a calendar for each of the studio's accounts.
  • Review every post (media, caption, privacy and interaction settings) and approve it explicitly before it can be published.
  • Publish approved posts at the chosen time through the official Instagram API, TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API.
  • Show the result of each publish (success, processing or error).

Who uses it

Postest is not a public service and has no sign-ups. It runs on the studio's own computer and is used only by the studio owner to manage the studio's own accounts.

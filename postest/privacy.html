Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Postest, the in-house publishing tool of Indaga Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. Postest is used only by the studio owner to publish content to the studio's own social media accounts. It has no public users and no sign-ups.

Information we access

When an account is connected, Postest accesses only what it needs to publish and to show results:

  • Instagram (Meta): basic profile information (account ID, username) and permission to publish media to that account.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), the creator's posting settings (privacy options, interaction settings, maximum video length) and permission to publish videos and photos to that account.
  • YouTube (Google): the channel ID and title, and permission to upload videos to that channel.
  • Access tokens issued by each platform, so Postest can publish on the schedule chosen by the account owner.

How we use it

Only to publish content the account owner has reviewed and approved, at the time they chose, and to show whether each publish succeeded. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

All data stays on the studio's own computer. Access tokens are stored in the operating system's encrypted keychain. Post drafts, captions and publish logs are stored in a local database. Images that Instagram must fetch by URL are placed temporarily at an unlisted public address and removed after publishing.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Content is sent only to the platform it is being published to (Instagram, TikTok or YouTube), at the account owner's request.

YouTube API Services

Postest uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By connecting a YouTube channel to Postest you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Postest's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page (third-party connections).

TikTok and Instagram

Use of TikTok is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Use of Instagram is subject to the Instagram Terms of Use and the Meta Privacy Policy. You can remove Postest's access from TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions) and from Instagram (Settings, Apps and websites) at any time.

Data deletion

To delete all data Postest holds about a connected account, disconnect the account in Postest (this deletes its tokens and cached profile data immediately) or email indaga.studios@gmail.com with the account name. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Revoking access from the platform's own settings (links above) also stops all access immediately.

Retention

Tokens are kept only while an account is connected. Publish logs are kept for up to 12 months so the studio can track its own posting history.

Children

Postest is not directed at children and is used only by the studio owner.

Changes and contact

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date. Questions: indaga.studios@gmail.com.

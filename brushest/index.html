Drawings that
won't sit still.

Brushest is an iPad app for Apple Pencil where every line stays alive. Lines boil, wave, bounce and fly apart, without redrawing a single frame. Try it: draw on the paper below.

draw on me!

This page is a small sketch of the idea, running in your browser. The app draws with the iPad's graphics chip, with Pencil pressure and tilt.

Effects

Stack effects like stickers.

Pick one, then add another on top. Effects go on a whole layer or only on the strokes you select, and they loop cleanly. You can draw here too.

Animate mode

Frame by frame, when you want it.

Draw a new pose on each frame, like a flipbook. Onion skin shows the frames before in red and the frames after in green. Pause, pick a frame and add your own drawing to it.

3D

Push your layers into space.

Each layer is a flat sheet you can place in depth. Drag to look around. Strokes can also lie on the floor or stand on a wall, and the camera can sway during the loop.

Also in the app

A full little studio.

Brushes that feel real

Ink, Pencil and Marker react to pressure and tilt. Tweak one and it becomes your own brush. Pin your favorites.

Animated brushes

Scribble, Goop, Jelly, Neon, Sketchy and Wobble move from the first touch.

Layers

Up to 10, each with its own wiggle, effects, opacity and timing.

Shapes and color

Close a line and it fills. Separate line and fill colors, with recent colors kept.

Select and reshape

Tap or loop strokes, then move, resize, rotate, recolor, or erase them.

Trace your sketches

Bring in photos or scans, lock them, and draw over them.

Timeline

See every frame as a thumbnail. Drag a stroke's bar to change when it shows up.

Presets

Save a wiggle or an effect stack with a name, for one drawing or all of them.

Projects

A home screen with all your drawings, search and folders. Everything saves by itself.

Export

Share, save to Files, or AirDrop.
